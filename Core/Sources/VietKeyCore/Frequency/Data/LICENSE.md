# Word frequency tables

`english.txt` and `vietnamese.txt` are adapted from FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave
(https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords, commit 525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6),
whose content is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. FrequencyWords is built from the OpenSubtitles 2018
corpus (http://www.opensubtitles.org, distributed by OPUS, https://opus.nlpl.eu; P. Lison and
J. Tiedemann, "OpenSubtitles2016: Extracting Large Parallel Corpora from Movie and TV Subtitles",
LREC 2016).

Changes: counts converted to occurrences per billion; English filtered to lowercase words that
contain a Telex key; Vietnamese filtered to syllables with a diacritic and keyed by their Telex
keys. Generated by `Tools/make-frequency-tables.py`.
